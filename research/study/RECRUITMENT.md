# Recruitment & screening

## Inclusion criteria

- Age ≥ 18
- Professional or serious personal software development experience (≥ 1 year)
- Has used at least one AI coding assistant (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Copilot, or similar) in the last 6 months
- Can attend a ~90 minute remote or in-person session
- Willing to be screen-recorded (optional; decline still allowed with note-taking)

## Exclusion criteria

- Cannot run a local Node ≥ 20 environment for the study machine (facilitator may provide a prepared VM)
- Requires proprietary employer code to complete tasks (study uses synthetic fixtures only)
